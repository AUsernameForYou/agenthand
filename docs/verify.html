Verify a staged photo

Every photo agenthand stages carries a disclosure label and a QR code pointing at this page. The code encodes this page's address plus the SHA-256 hash of the photo's fidelity receipt, carried in the URL fragment (the part after #). The fragment never leaves your browser: this page is static, and the lookup below runs entirely on your device.

Check a receipt yourself

Paste a receipt JSON below. The page removes the two annotation fields (verify_url and qr_sha256), canonicalizes what remains (keys sorted, compact separators, UTF-8 unescaped), hashes it, and compares the result against the hash in this page's address. A match means the receipt you pasted is exactly the receipt the QR code was minted from.
